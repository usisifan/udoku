udoku web 0.4.3 — more starting clues and late-game lives (2026-10-18 KST)

Levels 1–39: unchanged, 2 hints / 2 lives / 1 opening Ayadan.
Levels 40–59: 5 hints / 3 lives / 5 opening Ayadan; 10/28 = 35.714% maximum direct help.
Levels 60–79: 6 hints / 4 lives / 6 opening Ayadan; 12/32 = 37.5% maximum direct help.
Levels 80–100: 7 hints / 4 lives / 8 opening Ayadan; 15/40 = 37.5% maximum direct help.
These ratios count every available hint once plus starting clues; lives are not
revealed answers and do not contribute to this ratio.

All previous opening cells remain in the deterministic selection. New openings
are in distinct color regions, cover all four quarters, and span at least half
the board on each axis. Puzzle data, IDs, hashes, solutions, save format/key,
character assets, and completion badges are unchanged. Start-page life/chance
copy remains removed.

Compatibility: existing v0.4.2 hint limits do not increase, so hint uses are not
refunded. Active level 60–100 rounds gain the one-life allowance difference once,
while previous mistakes remain counted; lost rounds remain lost during engine
restoration. New starting cells are revealed in active rounds without spending
hints. Old notes, time and completion history are retained, except X notes on new
correct starting cells become Ayadan. Repeated reloads grant no further resources.

resource checks. 107 isolated Chromium/mobile UI checks passed, including

offline loading and narrow layouts. The 320px four-heart header was visually

All 61 later puzzles still have a propagation-only solving path within the hint
budget; maximum hints on verified paths are 3/5, 4/6 and 4/7 by band.
