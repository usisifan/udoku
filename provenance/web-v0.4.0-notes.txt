udoku web 0.4.0 — campaign assistance tiers (2026-10-18 KST)

Levels 1–39: 2 hints, 2 lives, 1 opening clue (unchanged).
Levels 40–59: 4 hints, 3 lives, 3 opening clues.
Levels 60–79: 5 hints, 3 lives, 4 opening clues.
Levels 80–100: 6 hints, 3 lives, 5 opening clues.

Openings keep the original given cell. Each additional clue comes from a different
color region. Selection prioritizes a new board quarter, then the maximum distance
from the closest selected clue. Stable solution-order ties make positions repeatable.
All 61 later levels have distinct-color openings; the 3-clue group covers 3 quarters,
and the 4/5-clue groups cover all 4. Every selection spans at least half the board in
both axes. Exact indices and measurements are in openings-v0.4.0.json.
Original puzzle data, IDs, hashes, solutions, and character images are unchanged.

Save compatibility: round version 4 stores hintLimit and lifeLimit. Legacy versions
1/2 had one hint; version 3 had two hints; all had two lives. Migration preserves
prior hint uses and mistakes, adds the allowance difference once, and reveals the
new starting cells. Other marks, elapsed time, historical completions/best results,
unlocks, theme, sound, browser save key and origin remain intact. An X on a newly
revealed correct opening cell becomes an Ayadan. Lost saves do not resurrect during
engine restoration; starting a retry follows the existing retry behavior. If the
new clues complete an active round, its result is recorded and the next level opens.

combinations. 89 isolated Chromium/mobile UI checks passed (web-v0.4.0.json), covering
band edges, dynamic counters/home/rules/retry text, full hint consumption, 3-mistake
loss, preserved history, no refill on reload, migration-completed rounds, offline

The duplicate footer rule sentence was removed; both navigation buttons remain.
Level 100 now distinguishes locked, incomplete and star-marked completion. The

UI runtime flags --no-sandbox --in-process-gpu --disable-gpu are test-only due to
