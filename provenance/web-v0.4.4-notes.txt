udoku web 0.4.4 — more distributed starting clues (2026-10-18 KST)

Levels 1–39: unchanged, 2 hints / 2 lives / 1 opening Ayadan.
Levels 40–59: 5 hints / 3 lives / 6 opening Ayadan; 11/28 = 39.286% maximum direct help.
Levels 60–79: 6 hints / 4 lives / 8 opening Ayadan; 14/32 = 43.75% maximum direct help.
Levels 80–100: 7 hints / 4 lives / 10 opening Ayadan; 17/40 = 42.5% maximum direct help.
These ratios count starting clues plus every available hint once. Lives are not
revealed answers and are excluded from the ratio.

All prior opening cells remain in the deterministic selection. New openings are
in distinct color regions, cover all four quarters, and span at least half the
board on each axis. Existing v0.4.3 hints and lives stay exactly as saved; active
rounds receive only the extra opening cells. X notes on those newly revealed
correct cells become Ayadan. Other marks, time, completed records and the level
100 completion badge persist. Reloading does not refill spent resources. Lost
rounds stay lost during engine restoration. A newly revealed final answer can
complete an active round and unlock the next level.

Verification: 25 Node tests passed, including 5,200 version-4 migration cases
from v0.4.0/1, v0.4.2 and v0.4.3, plus older-save and campaign checks. All 116
isolated Chromium/mobile UI checks passed, including saved progress, spent
resources, completion badges, offline loading, and 320px layouts. The 320px
level-100 view with 10 starting clues was visually inspected. Physical phones
were not tested. Test profiles were isolated from player data.
All 61 later puzzles have a propagation-only solving path within their hint
allowance; maximum hints on the verified paths are 3/5, 3/6 and 3/7 by band.
This verifies a path using the implemented deductions, not what every human
player will notice or every possible sequence of previously spent hints.

Puzzle data, IDs, solutions, hashes, save format/key, character assets, and
Windows 0.2.0 releases are unchanged. Only web/source in usisifan/udoku (ID
1423187970) is updated. Cache v0.4.4 activates normally after old game tabs close,
without forcing active games to reload. Other repositories are untouched.
